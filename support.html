Print at the size you chose

Export all pages as PDF, then select Actual Size or 100% in your printing app. Printer settings can resize a page. PaperSnips creates printable files; printing is handled by your printing app and printer.

Fit or Fill?

Keep whole preserves the full photo within its frame. Fill crops the photo to cover the frame. If a page is full, photos continue onto another page.

Free exports and Pro

The free version exports three distinct projects. Re-exporting the same project does not use another project. PaperSnips Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription. Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for your purchase to restore eligible entitlements.

Project storage

Projects are stored locally. Recently deleted projects can be recovered in History. Export files you want to keep before removing the app; uninstalling can remove local app data.
