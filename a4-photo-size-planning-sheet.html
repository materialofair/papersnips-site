A4 photo-size planning sheet

See how four small photo sizes fit your journal or scrapbook before making a layout. Print, check the scale, then cut out a blank frame to try on your page. No app, account or payment is needed.

Download A4 sheet (SVG)

Print one test sheet

  1. Use your browser’s Print command on this page. The print layout contains only the A4 sheet. Or open the downloaded SVG in a program that preserves its physical size.
  2. Load and select A4, portrait (210 × 297 mm). Choose 100% / Actual Size; turn off “Fit to page” and browser headers and footers. Check that the preview shows one complete page.
  3. Measure the 50 mm line and both sides of the frames with a ruler, between the centres of the thin outlines. Only cut after the measurements match. If they differ, check scaling and paper settings and print another test.

Some phone print dialogs do not offer scale controls. If yours does not, use a printing app or print service that can preserve the original size. Printer margins and scaling can change the result; exact physical output is not guaranteed.

This is an independently designed size-planning reference, not an app export, screenshot or physically tested print. You can use it on its own or with any photo-layout tool.
