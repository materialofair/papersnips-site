Your photos stay close.

Effective: October 6, 2026. This policy describes PaperSnips for iPhone and iPad, developed by qiao wang.

Photos and projects

PaperSnips processes the photos you choose on your device and saves project documents in local app storage. The app does not upload photos or project files to a developer server. No PaperSnips account is required.

Data collection and tracking

The developer does not collect personal data through the app. PaperSnips contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites.

Apple services

Apple StoreKit handles product information, purchases and purchase restoration. The app verifies Apple transactions to determine Pro access. Apple processes purchase-related information under its own privacy policy. Device backups and system services follow your Apple settings.

Export and sharing

Exports remain on your device until you save or share them using the system share sheet. A destination you choose may receive the exported file under its own privacy policy.

Support

If you contact wangnull@outlook.com, your email address and anything you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords or private photos.

Control your data

You can delete projects in History and remove items from Recently Deleted. Export files are managed in the destination where you saved them. Removing the app can remove its local storage.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if data handling changes. Privacy questions: wangnull@outlook.com.

隐私政策（中文）

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日。开发者：qiao wang。

你选择的照片在本机处理，作品保存在应用的本地存储中。应用不会把照片或作品上传至开发者服务器，无需 PaperSnips 账号。

开发者不通过应用收集个人数据；应用无广告、分析 SDK 或跨应用追踪。

商品信息、购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用核验 Apple 交易以确定 Pro 权益，购买相关信息由 Apple 根据其隐私政策处理。设备备份与系统服务遵循你的 Apple 设置。

导出文件只有在你选择保存或分享后才会发送至相应目的地，其数据处理遵循对应服务的隐私政策。

通过邮件联系支持时，你主动发送的邮箱地址和内容仅用于回复请求。请勿发送密码或私密照片。

你可在历史中删除作品，并在最近删除中移除；导出文件由保存位置管理。卸载应用可能删除本地存储。若处理方式变化，我们将更新此页面。隐私问题请联系 wangnull@outlook.com。
